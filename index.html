You’re invited to a Halloween party.

Come as the thing you’re afraid of.

An evening of costumes, cider and bad decisions in the dark. Bring your best fear and one story you’ve never told at a party.

When
Saturday, 31 OctoberDoors at 8 pm. Midnight is when it gets strange.
Where
The Old Print Shop, 13 Lantern LaneRing the bell twice. Use the back stairs.
Bring
A costume and a snackDrinks are on us. Pumpkins are in the courtyard.
Dress code

“Wear something that makes you a little uncomfortable, and then stand up straight.”

Costumes are judged at ten o’clock by a panel of three guests who have not yet been chosen. Categories include most frightening, most unsettling and best use of a bedsheet.

Phones stay in pockets after midnight. The house has no signal in the cellar, and we would like to keep it that way.

Will you come?

Type notes

Fraunces italic, weight 800, optical size 144 for the headline; the soft and wonk axes round and tilt the letters. Weight 300 to 500 for the quote and details.

Instrument Sans at 17 px with a 1.6 line height for small text and labels.

Try changing the opsz, SOFT and WONK values in the headline and watch the shapes change.
